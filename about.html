Her story

Why dentistry, and why gently

Dr. Rao earned her Bachelor of Dental Sciences from Rajiv Gandhi University in India and her Doctor of Dental Medicine from the State University of New York at Buffalo. She has been practising since 2014.

Her focus is general and cosmetic dentistry. Many people arrive anxious, often after a painful experience, and she takes that seriously.

She is experienced in treating children, adolescents and adults, including patients with dental anxiety. Every visit starts with a conversation, and treatment moves forward only when you are ready.

She is a member of the American Dental Association, the Maryland State Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry.
