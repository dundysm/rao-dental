# Emblem of Dentistry

Official black-and-white Emblem of Dentistry, American Dental Association.

- Image source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_official_emblem_of_dentistry_(bw)..jpg
- ADA history and usage: https://commons.ada.org/insignia/4/
- The ADA states the emblem is copyright free/public domain, may be used
  without permission, and is not an ADA logo or trademark.
- Downloaded October 5, 2026. Original JPEG retained without modification.

The footer uses CSS inversion and screen blending to display the emblem
as a muted light mark on the green background. This is a decorative symbol
of dentistry, not an additional membership or accreditation badge.
