Restorative dentistry

Crowns & bridges

Custom-fitted restorations that repair a damaged tooth or replace missing ones, restoring both function and appearance.

Book a consultation
Overview

Strength and shape, restored

A crown, sometimes called a cap, is placed over a natural tooth or an implant to protect it and restore its shape. A bridge fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the teeth on either side.

Both are fixed in place by your dentist, so they look, feel and work like your own teeth.

A crown suits a tooth that is damaged but still intact. A bridge suits a gap that has healthy teeth or implants next to it.

How it works

What to expect

You will always know what happens next, and you can pause at any point.

01

Examination

We assess the tooth or gap, take any X-rays needed and talk through your options.

02

Preparation

The tooth is gently reshaped for a proper fit and an impression is taken. Porcelain is matched to the colour of your teeth.

03

Temporary

A temporary restoration may be placed to protect the tooth while your permanent one is made.

04

Placement

Your crown or bridge is checked for fit and bite, then cemented in place.

Questions

Good questions, plainly answered

A crown restores a single damaged tooth. A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth using the neighbouring teeth as anchors. We will recommend one after examining you.
