Periodontal care

Diagnosis and treatment of gum disease, to protect the gums and bone that hold your teeth in place.

Book a consultation
Overview

Healthy gums, steady teeth

Gum disease starts when plaque builds up along and under the gumline. Early on it causes red, swollen or bleeding gums and is reversible with good care.

Left untreated it can progress, damaging the bone that supports your teeth and eventually leading to loose teeth or tooth loss.

Treatment focuses on removing the bacteria, helping the gums heal and keeping the condition stable with regular maintenance.

How it works

What to expect

You will always know what happens next, and you can pause at any point.

01

Assessment

We measure the gums around each tooth and take X-rays where needed to check bone levels.

02

Deep cleaning

Plaque and tartar are removed from above and below the gumline, with the area numbed for comfort.

03

Review

We check how your gums have healed and whether any areas need further care.

04

Maintenance

Regular cleanings, usually more frequent than standard checkups, keep things stable.

Questions

Good questions, plainly answered

Common signs are gums that bleed when you brush, redness or swelling, bad breath that persists, receding gums or teeth that feel loose.
