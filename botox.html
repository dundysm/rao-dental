Botox

Therapeutic and esthetic Botox, provided by a dentist certified in facial esthetics.

Book a consultation
Overview

Precise care, by someone who knows the face

Dentists train extensively in the anatomy of the face, jaw and mouth, which makes them well placed to provide Botox safely and precisely.

Esthetic Botox softens lines such as frown lines and crow’s feet. Therapeutic Botox can help relax overactive jaw muscles associated with clenching and grinding.

Botox is provided at Pikesville Dental Associates by Dr. Chaviva Golombeck, who is certified in therapeutic and esthetic Botox and a member of the American Academy of Facial Esthetics.
