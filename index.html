Meet Dr. Rao

General and cosmetic dentistry, gently done

Dr. Sumanya Venkat Rao is a general dentist who has been in practice since 2014, with a focus on general and cosmetic dentistry. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine at the State University of New York at Buffalo.

She is experienced in caring for children, adolescents and adults, and especially for patients with dental anxiety. She explains each step before it happens, lets you set the pace, and never rushes a conversation.
